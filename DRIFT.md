# Drift from upstream abuzucom/agents

`abuzucom/agents` is the upstream policy source. This repository adopts its
complete gate set, checker suite, test suite, hook registrations, CI workflows,
supporting documents, and synchronized copies.

## Adopted

- The complete 21-rule policy in `AGENTS.md` with verified project orientation.
- All portable checker scripts under `scripts/` with complete test coverage.
- All hook gates under `hooks/` with complete multi-client configurations
  under `.agents/`, `.claude/`, `.codex/`, and `.gemini/`.
- All supporting policy documents under `docs/agent-policy/`.
- `docs/gate-threat-model.md` threat model documentation.
- Reusable and push workflows under `.github/workflows/`.
- Templates for handoffs, security policy, and contributing guidance.
- Sync, lint, test, identity, and changelog targets in `Makefile`.
- Pre-commit hooks for sync, branch-name, and git-identity checks.
- Local synchronized tool copies generated by `scripts/sync.py`.

## Locally adapted

- `AGENTS.md` omits the upstream source-repository orientation block. The
  repository-specific `Commands`, `Do not touch`, `Architecture`, `Gotchas`,
  and `Read before touching` sections carry verified project orientation.
- `scripts/sync.py` accepts adoptable policy without source-repository markers.
- `tests/test_policy_distribution.py` asserts absence of source-repository markers.
- `adopters/1a2n-video-curator.md` records this repository's adoption state.
- `.claudeignore` includes `.foucault/` and duplicate tool instruction copies.

## Not adopted

- Upstream source-repository-only orientation in `AGENTS.md`.
- Upstream `adopters/` records for other repositories.

These omissions do not remove an applicable policy rule or enforcement gate.

## Foucault integration

This repository integrates the `abuzucom/foucault` PR security review pipeline:
- `AUDIT.md` is adopted verbatim and loaded by CI workflows.
- `security-review-pr.yml`, `security-review.yml`, and
  `immutable-conflict-check.yml` implement the PR review gate.
- `ci/build_pr_case.py`, `ci/call_model.py`, `ci/run_model_command.py`, and
  `ci/model_providers.json` support the model-based review.
- `scripts/check_compliance_tree.py` and `scripts/check_pr_review_response.py`
  support immutable tree scanning and response validation.
